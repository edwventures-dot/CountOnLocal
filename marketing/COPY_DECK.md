# Count On Local Approved V1 Copy Deck

## Marketing site

### Provider hero

**Eyebrow:** Neighborhood businesses start small.  
**Headline:** Start a business where you live.  
**Body:** Create a professional service page, get recurring customers nearby, and run your schedule, payments, and route from one simple dashboard.  
**Primary CTA:** Start my business  
**Secondary CTA:** See how it works

### Customer hero

**Headline:** Good help is closer than you think.  
**Body:** Subscribe to useful neighborhood services from local providers and manage everything without another round of texts, cash, or reminders.  
**CTA:** Find local help

### Guardian hero

**Headline:** They run the business. You stay connected.  
**Body:** Providers under 18 use a guardian-linked account with built-in service controls, payment requirements, and privacy protections.  
**CTA:** How guardian accounts work

## Provider onboarding

### Welcome
**Headline:** What could you do for your neighbors every week?  
**Body:** Pick one useful service. We will help you turn it into a page people can subscribe to.  
**CTA:** Pick my service

### Age gate
**Headline:** First, how old are you?  
**Body:** Count On Local is for providers 13 and older. If you are under 18, a parent or legal guardian will need to connect before you can accept paid customers.

### Guardian invitation
**Headline:** Bring in your guardian.  
**Body:** You can keep building your business while they complete their part. You just cannot publish paid services until the connection is approved.  
**CTA:** Send invite

### Business name
**Headline:** Give the business a name.  
**Helper:** Use your name, your neighborhood vibe, or something completely your own. Skip words like LLC unless that is actually your legal business structure.

### Pricing
**Headline:** What is your work worth?  
**Helper:** You set the service price. Count On Local does not take a cut from the price you set. Customers pay a separate platform fee.  
**Callout:** Set your price. Keep your price.

### Service area
**Headline:** Keep it close.  
**Body:** A tight route usually beats a big one. Choose the area you can serve reliably. Your private home location is never shown on your public page.

### Capacity
**Headline:** How many customers can you handle?  
**Body:** Start with a number you know you can deliver every week. You can raise it later.

### Publish
**Headline:** Your business is ready for neighbors.  
**Body:** Share the page, print your flyer, and get your first recurring customer.  
**CTA:** Publish business

### First publish success
**Headline:** You have a business page. Now put it to work.  
**Primary CTA:** Make a QR flyer  
**Secondary CTA:** Copy my link
