## Payment / age requirements

- Stripe support: age requirement to create a Stripe account. As checked, Stripe states users must be at least 13; under 18 requires a legal guardian for account ownership/consent, with Express/Custom Connect support depending on country.  
  https://support.stripe.com/questions/age-requirement-to-create-a-stripe-account

- Stripe services agreement, age restrictions.  
  https://stripe.com/legal/ssa

- Stripe US connected-account verification changes include a legal guardian relationship and consent fields when the representative is a minor.  
  https://support.stripe.com/questions/us-connected-accounts-accepting-card-payments-verification-requirement-changes

## Child privacy

- FTC COPPA materials / rule. The StreetStart design avoids under-13 provider accounts in V1 rather than building a child-directed data-collection flow.  
  https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule-coppa

## Youth work / hazardous work

- U.S. Department of Labor child labor / hazardous occupation guidance. StreetStart's product rules are intentionally more conservative than treating every legally possible activity as a marketplace-allowed activity.  
  https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/youthrules
